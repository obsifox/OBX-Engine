# v0.96 — Advanced Systems: Multiplayer, Open World, AI, Rendering & Profiler

**Tag:** `v0.96.0` · **Date:** 2026-09-28 · **Scope:** multiplayer · world · ai · rendering · profiler

The Advanced block of the roadmap: multiplayer lobby/rooms/matchmaking with anti-cheat
hooks, open-world async streaming with HLOD and occlusion plus seasonal world
simulation, enemy and wildlife AI brains, advanced rendering primitives (height fog,
foliage instancing, decals, water waves) and a full profiling toolkit.

## What's inside

- **@obx/multiplayer (4 tests)** — `Lobby` (join/leave, ready-state, ordered chat),
  `RoomManager` (create/join/leave with capacity, host hand-off, private rooms, room
  limits), `Matchmaking` (skill-sorted tickets, size + skill-range matching, cancel),
  `AntiCheatHooks` (validator registry with exception-safe auditing, per-player
  violation logs, reset).
- **open world (4 tests)** — `AsyncStreamer` (priority queue, per-tick load budget,
  duplicate/drop accounting, unload with asset hand-off), `HlodSystem` (distance-band
  LOD resolution), `OcclusionGrid` (cell occluders + line-of-sight queries),
  `WorldSimulation` (seeded seasons, population drift, procedural world events).
- **advanced AI (3 tests)** — `CombatBrain` (utility scoring over engage/flank/flee/
  reload/idle with distance falloff, threat, ammo and health), `WildlifeBrain` (seeded
  flee/graze/herd/wander decisions).
- **advanced rendering (4 tests)** — `HeightFog` (exponential height-falloff factors +
  color shading), `FoliageInstancer` (seeded scatter into cell batches with visibility
  queries), `DecalProjector` (project/overlap/remove), `WaterSurface` (sum-of-waves
  height, finite-difference normals, foam threshold).
- **@obx/profiler (5 tests)** — `CpuProfiler` (begin/end spans with open-span guards,
  aggregate report), `MemoryProfiler` (current/peak/allocation counts), `ChannelTracker`
  (named metric aggregation for asset/network/physics/script), `FrameDebugger` (frame
  capture with events, draw-call + triangle statistics), `ProfileReport` (combined JSON
  bundle).
